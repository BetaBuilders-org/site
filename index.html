Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

BetaBuilders exists to turn courageous ideas into working products while giving volunteers a place to learn, ship and shine. We rally builders and founders around a single goal: launch something that matters.

Our Vision

We imagine a world where talent and ambition are all you need to build a startup — funding optional. Every volunteer leaves with new skills, meaningful connections, and a portfolio piece they're proud of.

Founders We Support

We support startup founders who are committed to learning, building, and leading with purpose.

Traits We Look For

  • ✓ You want honest feedback, and you act on it.
  • ✓ Able to adapt thinking when presented with new insights.
  • ✓ You're solving a problem real people have.
  • ✓ Energetic and open to working with others collaboratively.

What We Teach

  • ★ First principle thinking — questioning assumptions to build from the ground up.
  • ★ Thinking from a business perspective — not just tech for tech's sake.
  • ★ Leadership — inspiring and enabling others to join your mission.
